The cover plate sets the temperature of the brand. A single risograph composition — layered violet ink, coarse grain, present and unhurried.

Abstract forms on a cool canvas.

  • Layer organic forms; let overlapping ink mix new violet tones.
  • Crop tight at 14 px, keep the coarse print grain visible.
  • Never composite the orb mark into an art plate.

Lead with the heading.
Earn the silence.

  • Every section opens with its own headline. No eyebrows, no all-caps lead-ins above an H1 or H2.
  • Mix paper and veil surfaces for rhythm. Reach for a second hue only when a status genuinely demands one.
  • Reserve depth — a soft 24 px violet shadow — for the cards that actually carry weight.
  • Never drop in stock or glossy 3D imagery. The grain is the argument.

The orb is the second voice of Marque.

A half-cut sphere, drawn in concentric indigo lines. It marks chapter heads at 32 px, returns at 96 px on signature cards, and fades into the royal block at 12 % white. Never used as wallpaper, never tinted off-brand.
